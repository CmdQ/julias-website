## Building bridges with language

I believe there is often a much greater overlap between us and the person we're talking to than we think. Yet the stories we tell ourselves about the world can create deep divides.

One of my great life missions — especially in the context of effective climate communication — is to develop tailored narratives that open up space for action, make common ground visible and inspire people to embrace complexity and explore it with curiosity.

## Public Speaking
I speak about hope as a practice, effective climate communication and how we can cultivate collective imagination. My talks weave together research, storytelling and practical tools — so that inspiration leads to real momentum.

Formats: Keynotes, impulse talks and panel contributions

Languages: German and English

Planning an event? Tell me about your audience and your goals, and I'll craft a talk that's the perfect fit.


## Training & E-Learning

I develop learning formats that spark curiosity and deliver fresh impulses — formats that move people and offer direct pathways to action.

For many years, I have been working with Greenpeace Germany. I have designed e-learning programmes for them on topics such as nonviolent communication, public relations, and engagement in the financial and agricultural transition.

I also specialise in workshop formats using creative and contemplative methods, particularly Active Participatory Futuring.

I'm also happy to give your learning materials a special visual touch with my illustrations — making complex topics more accessible and ensuring that what's been learned stays memorable.

Planning a training programme or workshop series? Whether online or in person — I support you from needs analysis to delivery.
